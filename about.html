Our story

A neighbourhood practice, rebuilt around the patient

Dental Studio is a new name for a long-established practice — the same principals, the same chairs, a considered rethink of everything around them.

We have looked after Leppington families on Emerald Hills Boulevard for years. In that time the suburb has grown quickly, and so has what our patients ask of us — implants, veneers, aligners, sedation, care for children and grandparents in the same week.

The rebrand to Dental Studio marks that shift. It is not a change of ownership or of team. It is a commitment to a higher standard in the things patients actually feel: appointments that run on time, treatment explained before it is recommended, written quotes, and a practice that looks and feels calm.

Three principal dentists lead the practice, supported by oral health therapists, dental assistants and a front-of-house team who will know your name by your second visit.

How we work

We explain before we recommend
You will see what we see and understand why a treatment is being suggested before it is booked.
We quote in writing
Itemised plans with treatment item numbers, so you can check your health fund cover.

The same dentist follows your care through, from plan to review.
