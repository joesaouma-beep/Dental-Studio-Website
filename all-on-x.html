Full-arch implant treatment

All-on-X — a full arch of fixed teeth, on four to six implants

One planned procedure that replaces a whole arch with teeth that are screwed in place, cleaned like your own, and do not come out at night.

Book a consultation Payment plans

All-on-X is a single treatment plan for an entire upper or lower arch. Four to six implants are placed in the areas of strongest bone, and a fixed bridge of teeth is secured to them. The X is the number of implants your case needs — most often four, sometimes six where the bite or bone volume calls for it.

Because the bridge is anchored to implants rather than resting on the gum, it does not rock, does not need adhesive, and does not cover the palate. Most patients describe eating and speaking as close to natural teeth.

Every case begins with a 3D scan. We plan the implant positions on screen before any surgery is booked, and you see the plan and the projected result before you commit to anything.

How the treatment runs

Stage one
Consultation and 3D scan

A full examination, photographs and a cone-beam 3D scan. We assess bone volume, nerve and sinus position, and your bite, then plan implant positions on screen.

One appointment, about 60 minutes
Stage two
Written plan and quote

You see the digital plan and the projected result, with an itemised quote and item numbers for your health fund. Nothing is booked until you are satisfied.

Within a week of the scan
Stage three
Surgery and immediate teeth

Any remaining teeth in the arch are removed, four to six implants are placed, and a fixed provisional bridge is fitted the same day in most cases. Local anaesthetic with sedation available.

One day, three to four hours
Stage four
Healing and review

The implants integrate with the bone while you wear the provisional bridge. We review you at set intervals and adjust as the tissues settle.

Three to six months
Stage five
Final bridge

The definitive bridge is made to the shade and shape you approved, in zirconia or titanium-reinforced acrylic, and secured to the implants.

Two to three appointments
Ongoing
Maintenance

A professional clean and check of the bridge and implants, with the bridge removed for cleaning as required.

Every six to twelve months
Who it suits
—A full arch of failing, loose or heavily broken-down teeth.
—Long-term denture wearers tired of movement, adhesive and palate coverage.
—Advanced gum disease where the teeth in an arch cannot be saved.
—Patients who want a fixed result they clean like natural teeth, not a removable one.
—Enough bone in the front of the jaw to anchor implants — the scan confirms this.
Worth knowing

All-on-X is not for everyone. Smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, untreated gum disease and insufficient bone all affect suitability and long-term success. The 3D scan tells us honestly whether it is the right treatment for you — and we will say so if it is not.

Questions patients ask first

Will I leave without teeth?
No. In the great majority of cases a fixed provisional bridge is fitted the same day, so you leave with a full arch of teeth in place.
Is the surgery painful?
It is done under local anaesthetic, and sedation is available if you would rather sleep through it. Most patients describe the days afterwards as swollen and tender rather than painful, managed with ordinary pain relief.
How long does it last?
Implants themselves are designed to be permanent, and the bridge is a wearing part that may need refurbishment or replacement over many years. Maintenance appointments and not smoking make the largest difference.
Can I claim on my health fund?
The surgical and prosthetic items usually attract rebates, though the amount depends on your fund and policy. Your quote carries item numbers so you can confirm the figure before starting.
Do I have to decide at the consultation?
No. You take the plan and the quote home. We are happy to review it with you again, and to explain what the alternatives would involve.
