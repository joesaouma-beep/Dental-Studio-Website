Orthodontics

Orthodontics & Invisalign

Clear aligner and orthodontic treatment to straighten teeth, close gaps and correct the bite.

Photo by Candid on Unsplash

Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners to move teeth gradually into a planned position. They are far less visible than fixed braces and can be taken out to eat and clean.

Treatment begins with a digital scan and a 3D plan showing the projected movement of every tooth. You will see a simulation of the expected result before you commit. Most cases run between six and eighteen months, with aligners changed every one to two weeks.

Retainers are essential afterwards. Teeth will drift back without them, and this is not optional.

What to expect

Digital scan and 3D treatment simulation at consultation.
Aligners worn 20 to 22 hours a day.
Reviews every six to eight weeks.
Retainers required indefinitely after treatment.
Complex cases may be better treated with fixed braces or referred to an orthodontist. Results depend heavily on wearing the aligners as prescribed.
