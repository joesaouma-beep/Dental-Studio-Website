Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth Whitening

Professionally supervised whitening, in the chair or with custom take-home trays.

Photo by Kamal Hoseinianzade on Unsplash

Whitening lifts staining from within the enamel using a professional-strength gel. Because it is supervised, we can protect your gums, control the concentration and stop if sensitivity becomes an issue.

In-chair whitening takes about ninety minutes. Take-home kits use custom-made trays and are worn for a set period each day over one to two weeks — slower, but often more comfortable and easier to top up later.

A check-up and clean is required first: whitening gel works on clean enamel, and any decay or gum problems need treating beforehand.

What to expect

Check-up and clean required before treatment.
In-chair around 90 minutes, or take-home over 1 to 2 weeks.
Temporary sensitivity is common and settles within days.
Custom trays let you top up in future.
Whitening does not change the colour of crowns, veneers or fillings, and results vary between individuals. Not suitable during pregnancy.
