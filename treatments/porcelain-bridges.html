Porcelain Bridges

A fixed replacement for a missing tooth, anchored to the teeth on either side of the gap.

Photo by Katarzyna Zygnerska on Unsplash

A bridge fills a gap left by a missing tooth using the adjacent teeth as supports. Unlike a denture it is fixed in place, so it does not come out and does not move when you eat or speak.

Bridges are a good option when the neighbouring teeth would benefit from crowns anyway. Where those teeth are healthy and untouched, an implant is often the more conservative choice, and we will say so.

Cleaning under the bridge with floss threaders or interdental brushes is important to its longevity.

What to expect

Two to three appointments over several weeks.
The supporting teeth are prepared for crowns.
A temporary bridge is worn while the final one is made.
Specific cleaning technique demonstrated at fit.
A bridge relies on the health of the supporting teeth. We assess these carefully before recommending one.
