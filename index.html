The studio

Modern family and cosmetic dentistry, under one roof

We built the practice around the parts of a dental visit people find hardest: the waiting, the not-knowing, the rush. Appointments run to time, findings are shown to you on screen, and nothing is booked until you understand why.

From a routine check-up to a full smile makeover, treatment is planned around what you need clinically and what fits your life — for children, parents and grandparents alike.

Anxious about treatment? Tell us when you book. We take it slowly, and sedation is available.

Our story

A neighbourhood practice, rebuilt around the patient

We have looked after Leppington families on Emerald Hills Boulevard for years. In that time the suburb has grown quickly, and so has what our patients ask of us — implants, veneers, aligners, sedation, care for children and grandparents in the same week.

The rebrand to Dental Studio marks that shift. It is not a change of ownership or of team. It is a commitment to a higher standard in the things patients actually feel: appointments that run on time, treatment explained before it is recommended, written quotes, and a practice that looks and feels calm.
